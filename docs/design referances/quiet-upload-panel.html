A status dot and a word

The speed pill shows a coloured dot, a word and the speed: ● Fair 0.8 MB/s. The word is always shown, at every width, so it still reads for colour-blind photographers and in bright venue light. The dot pulses only when Slow or Stalled. Try "Office fibre", "Mobile, like tonight" and "Slow connection" on the panel above.

The levels are absolute, not relative to the photographer's own best speed. A relative scale would show a 0.8 MB/s mobile connection as "healthy" because it's always that slow, which hides the real problem: a 2,000-photo wedding at 0.8 MB/s takes over 5 hours. Tonight's test lands on Fair, which is honest.

For the dev team

Rules

  • Speed = confirmed bytes over the last 10 s ÷ 10, recomputed every second. Confirmed means the PUT (or multipart part) returned 2xx. Never use raw xhr.upload.onprogress bytes for the speed figure; the browser reports bytes as they enter the socket buffer.
  • Per-file bars may still use onprogress, because they're only a sense of motion. The overall bar and the GB figure use confirmed bytes only, so they can't go backwards on a retry.
  • Time left = remaining bytes ÷ the 10 s speed, rounded: under 1 min → "less than a minute", under 60 min → "about N min", above → "about N h M min". Hide it for the first 10 s.
  • Connection health: status dot + word inside the speed pill, using the levels in the table above. Thresholds live in config. Never show the dot without the word. Accessible name: "Connection Fair, 0.8 megabytes per second".
  • "N at a time" shows the uploader's current adaptive concurrency, not a fixed number.
  • Slow connection: 10 s speed below 30% of the previous 60 s average for 10 s in a row. Clear it when the speed is back above 60%. Retrying files show a "Retrying" chip, never a reset to 0%.
  • Sizes: MB below 1,000 MB with one decimal; GB above with two decimals. Never "1084.0 MB".
  • Cancel confirms in the panel: "Stop N remaining uploads? The X already uploaded stay in this set." No browser dialog.
  • Tokens: Quiet v1.4 values unmodified.
