Network
Try it. Open the Muhurtham set, play the preview (runs 15× fast, stops at 1:30), then start the download and press "Drop connection" partway through.
Resume is the whole feature. A 4 GB download restarting from zero at 80% on 4G is the moment the client goes back to the Drive link. R2 serves range requests. The downloader must use them, and needs testing on Android Chrome over a weak connection before beta.
Hide a set in the studio sidebar (the eye icon) and it leaves the phone immediately. That's correction 2 below working end to end.

  • R2 master always. Stream is a derived copy made from R2. Switching never needs a re-upload.
  • Stream minutes are one pool per studio, shown under Storage, counted as stored minutes of streamed films, rounded up per film. They replace the 30-minute rule and the 2-hour cap.
  • Threshold is 45 minutes, a server constant. Check it against real cinematic cuts before fixing it.
  • Auto-assignment runs in upload order across sets. A film already streaming never drops to download because the pool ran out.
  • Preview is 90 seconds, starting near 10% of runtime, rounded to 30 s, made with ffmpeg at upload. The studio can move it in the drawer.
  • Upload asks nothing. When a batch contains a video, one hint line shows in the upload panel: long films go out as download with a preview, and any film can be switched later.
  • Hidden sets are excluded from the client gallery, and their films still count against stream minutes if they stream. The drawer says so.
  • No video goes through Guest Delivery. Photos only.
  • Tokens. Quiet v1.4 values unmodified.
